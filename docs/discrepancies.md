## 3. Inconsistencies inside a single filing

- **FY23**: Provisions on the balance sheet are 1,043k (pdf p.19); note 3 gives the dilapidations provision as 1,044k (pdf p.31). GBP 1k rounding difference; the balance-sheet figure is used.
- **FY21 (out of scope)**: FY22 accounts show FY21 exchange differences as (237) in note 6 (pdf p.34) but (239) in the KPI reconciliation (pdf p.5), so FY21 adjusted EBITDA (28,007) does not rebuild exactly (28,009). FY21 is outside the FY22-FY25 scope and not used.
- **FY22**: Employee categories are labelled 'Support staff' and 'Client service staff' (pdf p.35); from FY23 they are 'Administrative staff' and 'Store staff'. Treated as the same categories.
